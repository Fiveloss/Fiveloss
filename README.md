### 🎮 [Silverhand Game Finder](https://github.com/Fiveloss/silverhand-game-finder)

A Telegram bot that picks a game to play right now. Say it in your own words, *"like Hollow Knight, but easier, for a couple of evenings"*, and get three cards with the reasons they fit.

- 💬 your words, a mood button, or *"just not a shooter"*
- 🔎 a passport per game: real genres, 12 feel axes, praise and complaints
- 😌 a complaint can count for it: *"too slow"* is a plus if you asked for calm
- 🟢 honest about certainty · recent reviews apart from all-time
- 💰 prices in your Steam region · 🎮 Steam Deck status
- 🧠 free Gemini and Groq, optional: runs on heuristics without keys
- 🪶 one process, SQLite, under 300 MB of RAM

**[📖 Source](https://github.com/Fiveloss/silverhand-game-finder)** · **[🚀 Run your own](https://github.com/Fiveloss/silverhand-game-finder#self-hosting)**

### 🛠 How it's built

| | Stack | The interesting part |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ **Buys** | `Python` · `aiogram 3` · `SQLite` · `Pillow` · `ffmpeg` | No web3 library: logs, ABI and keccak are decoded by hand, and every chain fact is checked against mainnet before start. Prices come from the trade events themselves, so the public RPC gets a handful of calls every three seconds. |
| 🔴 **VPN** | `Python` · `aiogram 3` · `aiohttp` · `SQLite` · `Xray-core` · `Hysteria2` · `Caddy` | One process runs the bot, the web shop and the payment webhooks. Every payment is re-checked with the provider before a key is issued, and the subscription carries its own routing rules. |
| 🎮 **Game Finder** | `Python` · `aiogram 3` · `SQLite` · `Pillow` · `Gemini` · `Groq` | Review mining that keeps taste apart from quality: bugs and MTX are always a minus, *"too hard"* depends on what you asked for. Each free LLM does the one job it is best at within its quota. |

<details>
<summary><b>Habits shared by all three</b></summary>
<br>

- 🧪 offline tests for the places where a bug costs money or trust
- 🔁 idempotent by design: a payment, a buy post or a key is never issued twice
- 🎞 banners, cards and menu screens animated in code with Pillow and ffmpeg
- 🔒 secrets only in `.env`, and nothing stored that isn't needed
- 📦 one process, SQLite, systemd: cheap to host, easy to back up

</details>
